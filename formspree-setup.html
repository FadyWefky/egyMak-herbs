EgyMak Contact Form Setup

Setup Instructions:

  1. Go to formspree.io
  2. Sign up for a free account
  3. Create a new form
  4. Copy your form ID (e.g., "abc123def")
  5. Replace "YOUR_FORM_ID" in the form action above
  6. Set the email to receive submissions: egymak.mi@gmail.com
  7. Test the form
  8. Update the form ID in your React app
